### Biography & Contributions
Sri Lakshmipriya Teertharu, the twenty-fifth pontiff of Sri Shiroor Matha, was an ardent missionary of Tatvavada philosophy.

He traveled extensively across the Indian subcontinent on pilgrimages (ashesha teertha yatra), visiting sacred shrines, delivering discourses, and spreading the teachings of Acharya Madhwa far and wide.

He presided over the samsthana for ten years. In 1829 CE (Shaka 1751, Virodhi samvatsara), he attained Paramadhama at Udupi on Jyeshtha Shukla Trayodashi.
