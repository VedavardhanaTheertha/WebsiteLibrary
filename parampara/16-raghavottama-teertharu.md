### Biography & Contributions
Sri Raghavottama Teertharu, the sixteenth pontiff of Sri Shiroor Matha, was completely absorbed in the worship of the Supreme Lord and the delivery of spiritual discourses (Pooja-Pravachana Tatpara).

He guided the disciples and managed the samsthana with profound dedication for twenty-one years. In 1632 CE (Shaka 1554, Angirasa samvatsara), he attained Vishnu-pada on the banks of river Bheemarathi on Margashira Shukla Saptami.
