### Biography & Contributions
Sri Lakshminarayana Teertharu (the first pontiff of this name), the seventeenth pontiff of Sri Shiroor Matha, was reknowned for his unwavering commitment to pious conduct and strict ritual discipline (Sadachara Vrata-nishtha, Karmata Karmandi).

He presided over the sacred peetha for eighteen years, upholding the austere spiritual traditions of the lineage. In 1650 CE (Shaka 1572, Vikriti samvatsara), he entered Vishnu-loka (Vrindavana pravesha) at Udupi on Kartika Krishna Saptami.
