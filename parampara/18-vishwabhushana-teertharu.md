### Biography & Contributions
Sri Vishwabhushana Teertharu, the eighteenth pontiff of Sri Shiroor Matha, was a savant proficient in all traditional Shastras (Sarva-shastra Pandita).

He performed the celebrated pilgrimage spanning the length of India from Kanyakumari (Setu) to the Himalayas (Badari)—the Asetu Badari Yatra—spreading the teachings of Tatvavada along his path.

He ruled the dharma samrajya for twenty-two years. In 1672 CE (Shaka 1594, Paridhavi samvatsara), he attained Vishnu-pada on the banks of river Phalguni on Vaishakha Shukla Dvitiya.

### ಚರಿತ್ರೆ ಮತ್ತು ಸಾಧನೆಗಳು
