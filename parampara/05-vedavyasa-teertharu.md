### Biography & Contributions
Sri Vedavyasa Teertharu, the fifth pontiff of Sri Shiroor Matha, was an enlightened seer revered as Trikalajna—one who possessed divine insight into the past, present, and future.

He presided over the dharma peetha for seventeen years, upholding the Tatvavada philosophy and the rituals of the samsthana. In 1404 CE (Shaka 1326, Tarana samvatsara), he attained Paramadhama at holy Kashi Kshetra on the auspicious day of Chaitra Shukla Pratipat (Yugadi).
