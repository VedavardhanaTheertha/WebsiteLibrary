### Biography & Contributions
Sri Vedabhushana Teertharu, the eleventh pontiff of Sri Shiroor Matha, was a preeminent scholar of unmatched philosophical depth (Pandita Prakanda).

He decorated the dharma peetha for sixteen years, fostering scholarship and devotion. Having groomed and appointed his worthy disciple Sri Srinivasa Teertharu to the peetha, he journeyed to holy Sri Mushnam Kshetra (the sacred abode of Lord Bhu-Varaha), where he attained Paramapada in 1523 CE (Shaka 1445, Svabhanu samvatsara) on Jyeshtha Krishna Shashthi.
