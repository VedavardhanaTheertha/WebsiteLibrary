### Biography & Contributions
Sri Raghava Teertharu, the ninth pontiff of Sri Shiroor Matha, was an elevated ascetic dedicated to silent contemplation and austere penances (Maha Tapasvi).

He occupied the patta peetha for seventeen years, diligently guiding disciples in the principles of Tatvavada. In 1484 CE (Shaka 1406, Krodhi samvatsara), he made his ultimate soul offering (atmarpana) at holy Kashi Kshetra on Vaishakha Krishna Tritiya.
